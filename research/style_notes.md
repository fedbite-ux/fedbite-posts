# Style notes

## 2026-10-30
- Contrast hooks resonate: strong data vs. weak market reaction (growth beat, yet stocks fall).
- Single big number plus a consequence (3.4% GDP, 3.6% PCE, Alphabet worst day) beats abstract framing.
- Calm, factual tone with a clear "what changes next" works; avoid hype words.
(Note: trend study used search summaries only; CNBC front page was not fetchable.)
