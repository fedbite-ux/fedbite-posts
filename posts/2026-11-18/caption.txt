Nvidia earnings: revenue hit $115.8 billion, above forecasts, after the stock market sold off on Tuesday, Nov 17.

What happened: Nvidia reported fiscal third-quarter revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103% from a year earlier, with data center revenue of $107.6 billion, up 110%. Analysts, per an LSEG consensus reported by CNBC, expected about $109.1 billion. The company guided to about $130 billion for the next quarter, plus or minus 3%. Shares rose nearly 4% after hours.

Why it matters: the report came after a rough session. The Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 848 points, or 1.6%, the S&P 500 fell about 1.0% and the Nasdaq Composite fell about 0.7%, as chip stocks sold off ahead of the results. The 10-year Treasury yield was near 5.1%. Strong results are a fact; how the market reacts in regular trading on Wednesday is not yet known.

What to watch next: Fed meeting minutes (Wed, Nov 18, 2 p.m. ET), Nvidia's first regular session after the report, the Fed's Dec 8 to 9 meeting, and the Dec 11 US government funding deadline.
