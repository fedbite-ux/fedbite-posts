Stock market today: Nvidia fell about 5.9% on Nov 24, a day after a 31.5% surge, and the Nasdaq dropped 1.5% as AI stocks pulled back and Fed rate hike bets stayed high.

What happened: The Nasdaq Composite closed at 23,733.54 (down 1.5%) and the S&P 500 at 7,201.09 (down 0.5%), its eighth loss in nine days. The Dow rose 423 points (0.9%) to 50,018.70. CoreWeave fell 11.8%. Goldman Sachs said Monday's Nvidia jump leaned heavily on short covering, per Tradingkey.

Why it matters: Markets now price a December Fed rate hike as very likely (above 90%, per Yahoo Finance). Higher rates make borrowing costlier for companies building AI infrastructure, which is why these stocks react first. The 10-year Treasury yield was about 4.9%, and Brent crude rose to about $88.

What to watch: Nov 25, 8:30 a.m. ET, GDP (second estimate) and October income and spending data; Nov 26, US markets closed for Thanksgiving; Dec 8-9, Fed meeting.
