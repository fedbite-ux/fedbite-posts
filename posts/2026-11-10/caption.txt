Stock market rebound: Nasdaq +2.7% on Nov 9 as investors wait for October CPI inflation data.

US stocks rallied on Monday, Nov 9. The Nasdaq Composite rose 2.74%, the S&P 500 gained 2.35% to 7,549.94 and the Dow rose about 2.2%. Chip stocks led after last week's steep losses, and lower oil prices eased inflation worries. Nvidia was a notable exception, down about 4% amid a company probe.

Why it matters: the Bureau of Labor Statistics releases October CPI on Tuesday, Nov 10 at 8:30 a.m. ET. Economists expect core prices up about 0.2% on the month (an estimate; forecasts vary). No 12-month rate will be published because of missing data from the 2025 government shutdown. Markets also lean toward a Fed rate hike in December, which makes each inflation report matter more.

What to watch: Nov 10 CPI, Nov 12 30-year Treasury auction, Nov 13 producer prices (PPI).
