Week ahead for markets: CPI inflation on Tuesday, three Treasury auctions, and a Veterans Day break. Here are the 5 dates that could move stocks, Treasury yields and mortgage rates.

Tuesday, Nov 10: the Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) releases October CPI at 8:30 a.m. ET. Economists expect a 0.3% monthly rise (core 0.2%); estimates vary by source. Producer prices (PPI) follow on Friday, Nov 13.

Treasury sells 3-year notes Monday, 10-year notes Tuesday and 30-year bonds Thursday, each at 1 p.m. ET. Auction demand can influence yields, which feed into mortgage rates. Freddie Mac's 30-year average was 7.18% on Nov 5, down from 7.32% on Oct 22; the next update is Thursday. Markets price roughly 75% odds of a December Fed rate hike (an estimate, not a Fed decision).

Wednesday, Nov 11 is Veterans Day: no BLS releases and US bond markets are closed. Fed events this week include remarks from Michelle Bowman (Mon, Fri), Michael Barr (Thu) and Philip Jefferson (Fri).
