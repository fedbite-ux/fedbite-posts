Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigned on Nov 12, and the AI chip leader reports earnings Tuesday: what investors should watch.

Nvidia said its board accepted Huang's resignation as CEO and director on Nov 11, effective immediately. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO while the board searches for a permanent leader, and Harvey Jones was named board chair. CBS reported the stock closed down 4.3% at $166.19 on Nov 12.

Why it matters: Nvidia is a bellwether for AI spending and a heavyweight in major US stock indexes, so leadership changes can move chip stocks and the wider market. Earnings are due Nov 17 after the close, where the demand outlook may matter as much as the leadership question.

What to watch next: Nvidia earnings (Nov 17, after the close), US retail sales for October (Nov 17, 8:30 a.m. ET), and Fed minutes from the October meeting (Nov 18).
