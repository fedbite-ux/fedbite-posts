Inflation: October PCE hit 3.8% and a December Fed rate hike is now about 97% priced in.

The Commerce Department's Bureau of Economic Analysis reported on Nov 25 that the PCE price index rose 0.3% in October and 3.8% from a year earlier. Core PCE, which excludes food and energy, rose 3.3% year over year, above the 3.2% economists expected (LSEG poll, via Fox Business). Third-quarter GDP was revised up to a 4.3% annualized pace from 3.4%.

Why it matters: PCE is the Fed's preferred inflation gauge. Strong growth plus hotter core inflation leaves less room for the Fed to wait. Fed funds futures priced a 97% chance of a December hike (market-implied odds per CME FedWatch, Nov 25), which can keep mortgage rates, card rates and Treasury yields elevated. Stocks were volatile: the Dow ended up about 0.3% after a drop of more than 1,000 points, while the Nasdaq closed lower.

What to watch: Nov 27 early close for US stocks, Dec 4 November jobs report, Dec 8-9 Fed rate decision.
