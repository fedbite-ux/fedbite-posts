Stock market sell-off: the S&P 500 fell 1.9% on Monday, Nov 16, as Treasury yields stayed high and investors waited for Nvidia earnings.

What happened: the Nasdaq Composite fell 2.5% and the Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 739 points, or 1.4%. Nvidia dropped about 4%, its third straight daily decline since its CEO resigned, and it reports results Tuesday after the close.

Why it matters: news reports cite several pressures at once, including Nvidia's leadership change, new US limits on China's access to advanced AI chips, a 10-year Treasury yield above 5.1%, and market expectations of a Federal Reserve rate hike in December. Higher yields raise borrowing costs and make bonds a stronger alternative to stocks.

What to watch next: October retail sales (Tue, Nov 17, 8:30 a.m. ET), Nvidia results (Tue, after the close), Fed meeting minutes (Wed, Nov 18, 2 p.m. ET), and the Fed's Dec 8 to 9 meeting.
