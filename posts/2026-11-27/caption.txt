S&P 500 gained 3.9% this week while hot PCE inflation kept a December Fed rate hike in sight: FedBite Week in Review (through Wednesday's close, Nov 25).

The number of the week: the S&P 500 rose 3.9% to 7,182.78 after an 8.6% drop the week before. The Dow closed at 50,182.95 (+4.2%), the Nasdaq rose 4.3% and the Russell 2000 4.7%. Wednesday was a roller coaster: the S&P 500 fell as much as 3.2% on an AI-stock sell-off, then ended down just 0.3%.

Inflation did not cooperate. October PCE inflation ran 3.8% from a year earlier, with core at 3.3% (above the 3.2% economists expected), while Q3 GDP growth was revised up to 4.3%. Futures priced about a 97% chance of a quarter-point Fed hike in December (market-implied odds, CME FedWatch, Nov 25). For borrowers, the 30-year mortgage rate averaged 7.21% (Freddie Mac, Nov 25), down from 7.28% a week earlier but well above 6.23% a year ago.

What to watch next: Nov 27 early market close, Dec 4 November jobs report, Dec 8-9 Fed rate decision.
