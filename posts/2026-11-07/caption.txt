October jobs report: US employers added 165,000 jobs, and Fed rate hike odds for December jumped.

The Bureau of Labor Statistics said payrolls rose by 165,000 in October, well above the roughly 59,000 to 65,000 economists expected. The unemployment rate was 4.1%, and average hourly earnings were up 3.1% from a year earlier. August and September were revised up by 84,000 combined.

Why it matters: stronger hiring gives the Federal Reserve more room to raise interest rates again. Market-implied odds of a hike at the Dec 8-9 meeting rose from about 67% to roughly 85% right after the report, then eased to about 78%. Those are market estimates, not Fed decisions. Higher rates can keep mortgage and loan costs elevated.

What to watch next: October CPI inflation on Nov 10 (8:30 a.m. ET), Fed minutes on Nov 18, and the Fed decision on Dec 9.
