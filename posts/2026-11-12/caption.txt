Nvidia stock fell after hours as the board reportedly weighs removing CEO Jensen Huang, days before earnings.

What happened: The Wall Street Journal reported on Nov 11 that Nvidia's board is preparing to remove Huang and could meet as soon as that day. Nvidia declined to comment, and the report is not confirmed by the company. The board had already opened an independent investigation on Nov 8. After-hours declines were reported at roughly 3% to 10% depending on the outlet and time; CBS cited more than 8%, to $159.51. Before the news, the Nasdaq Composite closed up 1.6%, the S&P 500 up 1.3% and the Dow up 0.5% on Nov 11.

Why it matters: Nvidia is one of the largest stocks in the world and a bellwether for the AI trade, so leadership uncertainty can move the wider market. The company has said it will report fiscal third-quarter results on Nov 17 after the close.

What to watch: any Nvidia or board statement on Nov 12, US jobless claims and existing home sales the same day, and Nvidia's earnings on Nov 17.
