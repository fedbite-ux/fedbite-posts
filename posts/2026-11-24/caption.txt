Stock market today: S&P 500 jumped 4.7% on Nov 23, its best day since April 2025, as Nvidia surged after Jensen Huang's return as CEO.

What happened: Nvidia shares rose about 31% after the board reinstated Huang. Chip and AI stocks led the rally. The S&P 500 closed at 7,237.17 (up from 6,910.39 on Friday), the Nasdaq Composite gained 6.5% and the Dow about 3%. That is roughly half of the S&P 500's 8.6% loss from last week.

Why it matters: One company's leadership news moved the whole market, which shows how concentrated AI stocks have become. Analysts quoted by CNBC and NBC News noted that extreme one-day gains often come in downtrending markets, and Deutsche Bank's Jim Reid said similar volatility shocks have often taken 6 to 8 weeks to settle. The Fed is still weighing further rate hikes.

What to watch: Nov 25, GDP (second estimate) and October income and spending; Dec 4, jobs report; Dec 8-9, Fed meeting.
