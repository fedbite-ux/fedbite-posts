Caption:
Dow jumps nearly 1,600 points, its biggest gain of 2026, after Democrats win control of Congress in the midterm elections.

What happened: On Nov 4, the day after the vote, the Dow rose about 3%, the S&P 500 gained 1.6% to close at 7,689, and the Nasdaq added 1.4% (CBS News, CNN, CNBC). Solar and hospital stocks led. AI data center stocks lagged.

Why it matters: Capital Economics said the result is unlikely to change the near-term policy outlook much, since Democrats lack a filibuster-proof Senate and the president can veto bills. Strategists also cautioned against overreacting: LPL Research noted data center policy is set largely at the state level, and HSBC economist Ryan Wang pointed to the debt limit, expected to be reached in 2027, as the next big fiscal test. These are analyst views, not outcomes.

What to watch next: the October jobs report on Nov 6 (8:30 a.m. ET), the Fed's October meeting minutes on Nov 18, and the Fed's next rate decision on Dec 9.
