S&P 500 falls 8.6% in a week and enters a correction as the AI stock sell-off deepens (Nov 20, 2026).

The S&P 500 closed Friday at 6,910.39, down 3.7% on the day and 8.6% on the week. The Nasdaq fell 5.3% and the Dow 1.9%. The S&P 500 and Dow both closed more than 10% below their recent peaks, the common definition of a correction. Nvidia dropped about 14% on Friday and about 23% on the week, and the 10-year Treasury yield fell to about 4.91% as investors moved toward safer assets.

Why it matters: many retirement accounts and index funds hold the large technology and chip stocks that led the decline. At the same time, minutes from the Fed's October meeting showed almost all officials expect further rate hikes, so borrowing costs may stay high while stock prices are under pressure.

What to watch next: US stock markets are closed Nov 26 for Thanksgiving, the Fed meets Dec 8-9, and the November CPI inflation report is due Dec 10. Market expectations for a Fed hike are a forecast, not a decision.
