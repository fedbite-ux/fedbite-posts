S&P 500 fell 5.1% this week as the Fed's minutes pointed to more rate hikes: FedBite Week in Review (through Thursday's close, Nov 19).

Number of the week: the S&P 500 slid from 7,561.67 on Nov 13 to 7,175.83 on Nov 19. That is its sixth straight daily loss and 8.2% below October's record. The Dow closed below 50,000, and more than 90% of S&P 500 stocks fell on Thursday as worries about the AI boom kept spreading, even after Nvidia reported $115.8 billion in quarterly revenue on Nov 17.

The Fed story: minutes from the October meeting (released Nov 18) show the Fed held its rate at 3.75% to 4% in a 10 to 2 vote, with two officials preferring a quarter-point hike. Almost all participants see further hikes as likely. Market-implied odds (CME FedWatch, via CNBC) fully price a quarter-point hike on Dec 8-9. That is a market forecast, not a decision.

What to watch next: US markets are closed Nov 26 for Thanksgiving, the Fed meets Dec 8-9, and the November CPI inflation report is due Dec 10.
