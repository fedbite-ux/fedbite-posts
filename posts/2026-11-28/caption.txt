Dow posts its best week since 2022 as Treasury yields rise and Fed rate hike bets stay alive.

The Dow gained 5.5% this week, its biggest weekly gain since October 2022. The S&P 500 rose 5.6% and the Nasdaq 6.7%, rebounding from the prior week's sell-off. Friday's session ended at 1 p.m. ET after Thanksgiving: Dow 50,774.94, S&P 500 7,297.49, Nasdaq 24,129.50.

The catch: the 10-year Treasury yield rose to 4.94% from 4.86%. Reports say the latest PCE inflation data reinforced expectations of more Fed rate hikes. Higher yields raise borrowing costs for companies, mortgages and the government.

What to watch: the November jobs report on Dec 4, and the Fed's rate decision on Dec 9 (the meeting runs Dec 8-9). Whether the 10-year yield moves above 5% is another checkpoint.
